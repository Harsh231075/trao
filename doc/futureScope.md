# Visual Preview: New Candidate Study Schedule (`/schedule`)

Here is the complete visual design concept showing how the new **Day-by-Day Study Schedule** command center will look when implemented for candidates:

---

## 1. Study Schedule Command Center Dashboard

The main dashboard features an **Interview Countdown Banner**, a **3-Pillar Daily Action Checklist** (Concept Study, STAR Story Outlining, 15-Min Mock Practice), an **Overall Readiness Progress Ring**, and interactive day timeline cards.


---

## 2. Interactive STAR Method Builder & AI Feedback

When expanding a Behavioural / Leadership day card, candidate gets an integrated **Situation, Task, Action, Result** notes builder with instant AI feedback analysis (`Get AI Story Feedback`).


---

## Summary of Key Features:
- ⏱️ **D-Minus Interview Countdown**: Clear timeline counting down to interview day.
- 🎯 **Daily 3-Pillar Checklist**: Structured tasks per day (Read, Outline, Practice) — zero duplicate flashcards.
- 📝 **Embedded STAR Method Builder**: Type and save real personal stories directly into the schedule.
- ⚡ **Instant AI Feedback**: Real-time evaluation of candidate's STAR story metrics and impact.
- 💙 **Translucent Blue Glass Theme**: Matching Trao's modern UI design.
